    How it works

    Monitor

    Reads public chart and pool data for the pairs you view, plus the wallets already installed in your browser — balances, token holdings, price impact.

    Alert

    Volume spikes, fresh liquidity and holder clusters surface on your private feed within seconds, each graded by severity.

    Act

    Optional portfolio sync pushes a session summary to your control node. Anything further requires an explicit approval in your wallet.

    FAQ

    Do you ever ask for my seed phrase?

    No. Never, in any flow. All signing happens inside your own wallet extension.

    Which wallets are supported?

    Phantom, Solflare and Backpack natively; MetaMask accounts are read in view-only mode.

    Does Pump Patrol move funds on its own?

    No. Funds only move inside transactions you explicitly approve in your wallet.

    Which chains?

    Solana mainnet for portfolio sync and radar; EVM chains read-only.
